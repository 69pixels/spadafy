Why Spadafy?

A name that became a way of thinking

Spadafy started with a family name and a simple idea. Overnight, it turned into a verb. When someone made something better, we’d shout “you’ve Spadafied it!”. We liked the idea behind that: it wasn’t just about fixing things, but making everything work better together. This is exactly how we approach technology: untangle the complexity, fix what’s broken and make things easier and better for everyone.
