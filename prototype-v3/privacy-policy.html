    01Who we are

    This website is run by [Spadafy legal entity name] (“Spadafy”, “we”, “us”), a VDI and end-user computing consultancy with its registered office at [registered address].

    For the personal information described in this policy, we are the data controller: we decide why and how it is used.

    02What this policy covers

    This policy covers information we collect when you visit this website, contact us through it, or sign up for something on it, such as a Free VDI Health Check.

    It does not cover the data we process on behalf of our clients when we design, run or support their environments. That work is governed by our contracts with those clients, which set out how their data, and their users’ data, is protected.

    03What we collect

    What you give us

    When you fill in a form on this site, we collect what you type into it:

    • your name, company and work email address
    • the service you are interested in
    • anything you write in your message

    What we collect automatically

    Like most websites, our servers record basic technical information when you visit: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you open and when. We use it to keep the site running and secure.

    If you allow it, we also use analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. See Cookies.

    04How we use it

    • to reply to your enquiry and arrange the call, assessment or health check you asked for
    • to keep a record of our conversations with prospective and existing clients
    • to run, secure and fix this website
    • to understand which pages are useful and improve them, if you allow analytics cookies
    • to send you occasional updates, only if you asked for them, with an unsubscribe link in every email
    • to meet our legal obligations

    We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

    05Legal bases

    If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on these legal bases:

    Legitimate interests
    replying to business enquiries and running a secure website.
    Contract
    taking steps you ask for before we work together, such as a health check.
    Consent
    analytics and marketing cookies, and marketing emails. You can withdraw it at any time.
    Legal obligation
    keeping records the law requires.

    11Your rights

    Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

    • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
    • correct it, or delete it
    • limit or object to how we use it, including for marketing
    • move it to another organisation
    • withdraw consent you gave earlier

    Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have similar rights, including the right to know, delete and correct their information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

    To make a request, email [privacy contact email]. We will reply within the time the law allows, usually 30 days. If you are in the UK or EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

    12Health information

    When our work for a client involves PHI, it is handled under that client’s agreements with us, including a Business Associate Agreement where HIPAA applies.
